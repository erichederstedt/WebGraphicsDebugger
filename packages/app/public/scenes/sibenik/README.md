# Sibenik cathedral

Source: https://casual-effects.com/g3d/data10/research/model/sibenik/sibenik.zip
Archive: Morgan McGuire, Computer Graphics Archive, https://casual-effects.com/data/

Modeled by Marko Dabrovic, with holes corrected by Kenzie Lamar at Vicarious
Visions and high-resolution texture and bump maps painted by Morgan McGuire.
Original attribution is preserved in copyright.txt; archive assets are unchanged.

The demo renders diffuse colors and textures. Bump maps and glass transmission
are not rendered; glass materials appear opaque with their diffuse color.
